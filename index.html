How It Works

From the First Conversation to Your Finished Order

You may already have a detailed product specification or simply an idea you’d like to explore. Either way, we’ll begin by understanding your requirements and guide you through the steps involved in developing your fragrance.

Discuss Your Project

  1. Step 1: Share Your Brief

    1

    Tell us about your brand, target customers, intended market, fragrance preferences and expected order quantity. This gives us a clear starting point for your project.

  2. Step 2: Develop Your Product

    2

    Based on your brief, we’ll work through suitable fragrance samples and packaging options to shape a product that meets your expectations.

  3. Step 3: Approve Your Sample

    3

    Review the finished sample, assess the fragrance and packaging, and confirm the product details. Your approval provides the reference for the production stage.

  4. Step 4: Manufacture & Pack

    4

    Once approved, your product moves into manufacturing. We handle blending, processing, filling, quality checks and packing, preparing your finished order for collection or dispatch under the agreed terms.
